# SYSTEM CONTEXT – HOTEL + RESTAURANT POS

## 🎯 PROJECT OVERVIEW

This is a full Hotel Management + Restaurant POS system.

The system includes:
1. Online Hotel Booking (website)
2. Walk-in Customer Management (reception)
3. Restaurant POS System
   - Hotel guest orders (charge to room)
   - External customer orders (direct payment)

---

## 🧱 SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

- Frontend: Next.js (App Router)
- Backend: Fastify (Node.js)
- Database: PostgreSQL (Prisma)
- Storage: MinIO

Architecture style:
- API-based
- Modular backend
- Single database
- Shared services

---

## 🔗 SYSTEM MODULES

### 1. Room Management
- room_types
- rooms
- room availability

### 2. Customer Management
- guest profiles
- walk-in customers

### 3. Booking System
- online booking
- reservation
- availability check

### 4. Check-in / Check-out
- assign room
- track stay
- close stay

### 5. Restaurant POS
- menu
- orders
- billing
- charge to room

### 6. Billing & Payment
- invoices
- payments
- full system billing

---

## 🔄 CORE FLOW

1. Customer books online OR walks in
2. Reception assigns room
3. Customer checks in
4. Customer uses services or restaurant
5. Charges are added to invoice
6. Customer checks out and pays

---

## 🧠 DESIGN GOALS

- Clean architecture
- Scalable system
- No duplicated logic
- High performance
- Easy to maintain

---

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT RULES

- Booking ≠ Check-in
- Room must not be double-booked
- Orders must support:
  - hotel guest
  - external customer
- All charges must be unified in invoice

---

## 🚀 DEVELOPMENT APPROACH

- Build system module-by-module
- Start with core:
  1. rooms
  2. customers
  3. booking
  4. checkin
  5. pos
  6. payment
